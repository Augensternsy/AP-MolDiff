# PocketFlow: historical generated molecules

These 12 CSV files are unmodified copies of the generated-ligand data bundled in
[ProtoBind-Diff at commit 6bebe8915d9e3d94de0da6a61bb52dba6d50e48b](https://github.com/gero-science/ProtoBind-Diff/tree/6bebe8915d9e3d94de0da6a61bb52dba6d50e48b/data/pocketflow).
They are third-party public benchmark data, not molecules newly generated by AP-MolDiff.
