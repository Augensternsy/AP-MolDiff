# TamGen: historical generated molecules

The 12 CSV files under `generated_1000/` are unmodified copies of the generated-ligand data bundled in
[ProtoBind-Diff at commit 6bebe8915d9e3d94de0da6a61bb52dba6d50e48b](https://github.com/gero-science/ProtoBind-Diff/tree/6bebe8915d9e3d94de0da6a61bb52dba6d50e48b/data/tamgen).
They are third-party public benchmark data, not molecules newly generated by AP-MolDiff.

## Original local Vina evaluation records

The CSVs in `vina_evaluated_100/` are historical local docking outputs for the
benchmark molecules above, not an official upstream docking-data download or
a new generation/docking run. The repository owner authorized their public
research hosting. The reused source/terms review identified no explicit bar to
this noncommercial research redistribution. The original benchmark attribution
and CC BY-NC 4.0 conditions remain applicable to the embedded benchmark material;
these files are not relabeled MIT and no additional commercial clearance is claimed.
SMILES, Vina_Score, Ligand_Efficiency, original IDs, row counts and missing scores
are preserved byte-for-byte. A missing/nan score is not replaced by zero or an
invented success/failure explanation.

There are **12 original Vina CSVs, 1,200 rows** (100 per target). All 1,200
scored/attempted molecules are members of the retained generated molecule sets.
There is a **historical selection-batch difference**: the current candidate
selection snapshots overlap these original docking cohorts in only 719 of 1,200
molecules, with exact cohort agreement for four targets. Those current snapshots
match a later Correct-condition ablation cohort, whose records include 719 cache
reuses and 481 later docking entries. The later Correct/Shuffled table is NOT used
to replace these original CSVs. Original selected-list/command lineage is not
fully closed merely because the score files and generated molecules are present.
